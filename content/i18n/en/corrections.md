# Corrections

Spotted an error, a misread figure or a missing source? Open a report on our [public repository]({{depot}}) (“Issues” tab), stating the article and sentence concerned.

We check every report against the data. If the error is confirmed, the article is corrected and the correction is listed below, with its date.

## Published corrections

### 20 November 2026: measurement and wording errors

An audit of our data and articles found several errors. They have been corrected, and the program has been changed so they do not happen again.

- **False entries into the top 50.** When a game had not been measured the day before (lost reading or game unavailable), the program counted it as a new entry. Two daily reports made it their headline: [DONT SPLASH, 3 November](../articles/2026-11-03-dont-splash-quotidien/) and [Hyper Realistic Ragdoll, 18 November](../articles/2026-11-18-hyper-realistic-ragdoll-quotidien/). These games were returning to the top 50 after a gap; the articles now say so. The number of entries was also corrected in the daily reports of 7 and 14 November.
- **Rises calculated on poorly measured days.** Changes now only compare days on which the game was measured at least 18 hours out of 24. The rise for Unstable SMP FFA published on 11 November no longer holds: that daily report is now about [Vanguards: Trail of the Saint](../articles/2026-11-11-vanguards-trail-of-the-saint-quotidien/). The rise of [13 November](../articles/2026-11-13-unstable-smp-ffa-java-pvp-quotidien/) was recalculated.
- **“In a week” headlines.** Our changes compare one day’s average with the average of the previous seven days, not a change over a week. Eight headlines were reworded.
- **Peaks presented as player counts.** Headlines now say “a peak of … players”.
- **“Stays on top” without a known previous rank.** The daily reports of 22 October and 6 November were reworded.
- **Incomplete days not flagged.** The daily reports of 4 and 5 November now state that the day has only 17 readings out of 24.
- **One-hour peaks.** When a peak is more than three times the neighbouring readings, the article now flags it as a one-off and gives the game’s rank by daily average.
- **Duplicate translations.** Two old translations from 8 and 9 November, with no French original, were removed.
