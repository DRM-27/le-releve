## What we collect

**When you visit the site**: nothing that identifies you. No cookies are set. Audience measurement uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies or persistent identifiers: it counts page views, in aggregate.

## Why, and on what basis

- To send you the newsletter and monthly report you asked for. Legal basis: your consent, given when you sign up.
- To measure interest in our content, in aggregate, in order to improve it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

As long as you are subscribed. After you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the active list; it may be kept for up to three years in a suppression list, solely so that we never write to you again.

You can unsubscribe at any time in one click (link at the bottom of every email), and request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to the address above. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).
