On August 23, 2025, Roblox counted **{{ext.roblox.ccu_aout25}} players connected at the same time**, a record for the platform. The previous one, set in July, was {{ext.roblox.ccu_juil25}}. Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot drove this surge.

## Grow a Garden, the garden that changed everything

Launched on March 26, 2025 by an anonymous {{ext.gag.age_createur}}-year-old creator, Grow a Garden had only about {{ext.gag.ccu_avril}} concurrent players in April, when the studio Splitting Point bought into it. What followed was meteoric: about {{ext.gag.ccu_mai}} concurrent players on May 18, {{ext.gag.ccu_juin}} on June 21, then at least {{ext.gag.ccu_aout}} on August 23. It took {{ext.gag.jours_milliard}} days to reach its first billion visits, a speed record on the platform. These figures are those reported by IGN, PCGamesN and PocketGamer.biz, compiled on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden).

## Steal a Brainrot takes over

Launched on May 16, 2025, Steal a Brainrot first passed {{ext.sab.visites_juil}} visits in July. On August 23, a staged “admin war” between its developers and those of Grow a Garden boosted both games. Steal a Brainrot then took the record from its rival: {{ext.sab.ccu_sept}} concurrent players on September 13, then {{ext.sab.ccu_record}} in October 2025, according to PocketGamer.biz as cited by [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot).
