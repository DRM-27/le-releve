## What we don’t know yet

Showing Halloween in a game’s name doesn’t tell us whether the update brings in players. Our hourly tracking began on October 21: in the coming days, we will be able to compare these games’ traffic before and after October 31, game by game.

Method: we count games whose name, as displayed by Roblox, contains 🎃, “Halloween” or “HLWN”. A game running an event without flagging it in its title is not counted.
